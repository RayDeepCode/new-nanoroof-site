Clear or color?

Both start the same way: assessment, tune-up, cleaning and a Roof Record. The difference is how the roof looks afterward.

  • Clear: keeps your roof’s current color, for roofs that still look the way you want
  • Color: restores a roof that has faded, streaked or gone uneven

Choosing a color

  • See it in daylight: ask your contractor for a sample on your roof; screens shift color
  • Check your HOA: many communities approve roof colors, so confirm before you choose
  • Match the house: look at siding, trim, gutters and stone together
  • Stay close: a shade near the original keeps repairs blending in
