Shingle roofs: SPS System

NANOROOF SPS System

BaseWater-based silicone elastomer
SubstrateAsphalt shingles
ApplicationLow-pressure spray, in north-south and east-west passes
FilmPenetrates past the granules and bonds inside the asphalt shingle, and adds a surface layer
RecoatableYes, formulated to bond to itself
Application range40°F to 100°F. Rain before it cures dilutes it
Recorded on every jobMoisture readings, metered material applied and a test sample
Applied byIndependent NANOROOF Certified Contractors

Elastomeric caulk

TypeFlexible elastomeric caulk
Adheres toMasonry, wood, asphalt, metal, vinyl
ConditionsDamp surfaces and low temperatures
UseGaps, flashings, shingles, penetrations

Liquid flashing

TypeClear, brushable elastomeric caulk
UseChimneys, valleys, vents, flashing, gutters, sills, thresholds, siding
ApplicationBrush
