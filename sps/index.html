Silicone Preservation System

NANOROOF SPS

Every NANOROOF treatment is part of one system: SPS, our Silicone Preservation System. It’s a professional-grade, water-based, nano-enhanced silicone formulated for asphalt shingles, with no VOCs.

  • Water-based
  • No VOCs
  • Nano-enhanced silicone
  • On roofs since 2012

It goes in, and it stays on

Rejuvenators only soak in, and top coats sit on the surface where they crack and peel. SPS penetrates the shingle and encapsulates it.

Rejuvenators

Only soak in

They soak into the asphalt and leave no protective layer on top.

Top coats

Only sit on top

A film on the surface, where it can crack and peel.

NANOROOF SPS

Penetrates and encapsulates

It bonds inside the shingle and forms a protective layer on top. It doesn’t delaminate and doesn’t yellow over time.

What it protects against

SPS protects the shingle against UV, water, and algae and moss, without trapping moisture.

UV

Sunlight is what ages asphalt. SPS encapsulates the shingle to protect it.

Water

The cured silicone forms a protective layer against water and atmospheric moisture.

Algae and moss

The roof is soft-washed first, then protected against algae and moss.
