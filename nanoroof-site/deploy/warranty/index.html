What's covered

The warranty covers the treatment's useful life: the period in which the shingles can reasonably be expected to protect the roof structure underneath. Under normal weather, that means the treated shingle surface:

  • Resists water penetration
  • Holds granules in place
  • Resists UV degradation
  • Resists mold, algae and mildew

What isn't

  • Acts of God and natural disasters, including storm damage
  • Roof leaks, and failures of the structural roof system or its attachments
  • Failure of earlier coatings applied by others
  • Unforeseen environmental conditions
  • Damage from faulty application or inadequate repairs

The warranty applies when the products are applied according to the NANOROOF application protocol.

Making a claim

  1. If you find a problem, contact your dealer and Nano Products in writing within 5 business days.
  2. Include your address, warranty number and photos.
  3. Your dealer inspects the roof and checks it against your Roof Record.
  4. Covered areas are retreated or repaired under the warranty terms.
