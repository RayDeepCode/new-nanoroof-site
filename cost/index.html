What does roof preservation cost?

Most NANOROOF preservation jobs come in around $1.40 to $2.25 or more per square foot of roof. Your Free Roof-Life Assessment sets the actual price, and it varies by contractor and market.

Illustration

Example ranges by roof size

Roof area is larger than the floor area of the house: pitch and overhangs add to it. These are examples, not quotes.

1,500 sq ft of roofAbout $2,100 to $3,375 or more
2,000 sq ft of roofAbout $2,800 to $4,500 or more
2,500 sq ft of roofAbout $3,500 to $5,625 or more
3,000 sq ft of roofAbout $4,200 to $6,750 or more

Calculated from the typical range of $1.40 to $2.25 or more per square foot. The assessment measures your roof and confirms whether it qualifies before any price is final.

What moves the price

Two roofs of the same size can price differently.

Size and pitchMore roof area and steeper slopes take more material and more time on the roof.
Repairs firstLeaks, lifted flashing and damaged shingles are repaired before preservation. Preservation doesn't fix leaks.
CleaningAlgae, moss and grime are washed off before treatment, which adds a step on some roofs.
AccessTwo- and three-story homes, tight lots and complex roof lines take longer to work safely.
Your marketCertified Contractors are independently owned and set their own prices for their area.

Compared with replacing the roof

Preservation is for a roof that is still sound. If yours isn't, the assessment says so, and replacement is the right answer.

NANOROOF preservationAbout $1.40 to $2.25 or more per square foot. Includes the written assessment, the job record and a registered warranty.
Asphalt shingle replacementAbout $3 to $16 per square foot installed, depending on the shingle; national average around $12,000 a project, commonly $8,000 to $16,000.1

1 Third-party national averages, not NANOROOF prices: Fixr asphalt shingle cost guide, updated January 2026. Local prices vary.
