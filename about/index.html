About NANOROOF

We make roofs last longer, so property owners can put replacement money to better use.

Illustration

What we do

Most asphalt roofs are replaced because the surface has aged, not because the roof has failed. NANOROOF preserves roofs at that stage. The work is done by certified NANOROOF Certified Contractors.

That lets homeowners and property managers move roof replacement and repair budgets to other projects, with a warranty and a full record behind the roof they kept.

How the network works

NANOROOF products and the warranty come from NANO Products, LLC dba NANOROOF. The work is done by independent NANOROOF Certified Contractors who are trained on the NANOROOF Protocol and document every job. Dealers also give back locally, including roof preservation for neighbors in need.

What we believe

Most roofs are replaced on a schedule, not because they failed. We think that habit costs homeowners and the planet more than it should, and that the answer is proof, not promises.

Condition over age

A roof should be judged by how it’s holding up, not the year it went on. So we measure and record it.

Condition, measured

The whole roof

Every job starts with the structure, flashings, ventilation and workmanship, and we say no when a roof isn’t a candidate.

The roof as a system

Proof on record

Photos, readings, material used and a test sample, registered to the address in the Roof Record.

Our mission

To make the built environment last longer with professional-grade materials, and to cut the waste of replacing what still works.

That means fewer roofs torn off on a schedule, more value for the people who protect property, from owners to contractors to insurers, and an industry where condition counts more than age.
