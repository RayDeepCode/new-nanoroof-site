Roof-Life Assessment

A Free Roof-Life Assessment that tells you, in writing, whether your roof can be preserved, what it needs first, and what preservation and replacement would each cost.

Book an assessmentWhy we check the whole roof
Illustration

What the contractor checks

25-point inspection scored out of 250, with ULEA Levels 1–7.

  1. Shingle surfaceFading, brittleness and surface cracking
  2. Granule lossHow much is left, and where it's thinning
  3. Missing or damaged shinglesCracked, curled, lifted or missing
  4. FlashingsChimneys, walls, skylights and valleys
  5. PenetrationsPlumbing vents, fans and mounts
  6. DeckingSoft spots and sagging that rule preservation out
  7. ValleysWear and debris build-up
  8. Algae, moss and lichenWhat needs washing off first
  9. Gutters and drainageWhere water goes once it leaves the roof
  10. Attic ventilationHeat and moisture that age shingles from below
  11. Eaves and soffitsWater damage and rot
  12. Leak threatsPlaces water is likely to get in next

What you get

A clear answer, either way.

  • Qualifies or not: and the reason, in plain language
  • Photos: of every slope and every problem found
  • Work needed first: repairs and tune-up items, priced separately

When to book one

  • Your roof is 8 years old or more and starting to fade
  • You're seeing granules in the gutters
  • Algae streaks or moss have appeared
  • You're planning to sell, or an insurer has asked about roof age
  • After a major storm

Three possible answers

Every assessment ends with one of these, in writing.

Qualifies

Sound decking, enough granules, no failed flashings. You get the preservation price next to the replacement price.

Qualifies after repairs

A few items need fixing first. They’re listed, photographed and priced separately, so you can decide.

Not a candidate

Soft decking, too little granule left, or a roof too far gone. The report says so, and why.

What the report looks like

Plain language, photos of every slope, and the numbers side by side.

  • The verdict: qualifies, qualifies after repairs, or not a candidate
  • Findings: each problem with a photo and its location
  • Repairs first: itemized and priced
  • Two prices: preservation and replacement
